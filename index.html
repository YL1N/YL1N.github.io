About

I am an MPhil student in Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), where I am a member of the Di² Lab (Deep Interdisciplinary Intelligence Lab) led by Prof. Yutao Yue. I received my bachelor's degree in Educational Technology from Zhejiang University of Technology in 2025. Since October 2025 I have also been a research assistant at the Shanghai Innovation Institute and East China Normal University, where I am a member of the ZeroLoss Lab led by Prof. Fei Tan and work on InnoSpark, a Chinese educational large language model. I am looking for PhD positions starting in 2028.

My research follows two main lines, AI for education and the safety of large language models. I am also exploring how language models relate to human language and cognition.

Research

AI for education

I study how large language models can represent learners, support teachers, and be evaluated for real teaching use. HACHIMI and ADE build student personas and training data for this purpose, ELBench evaluates education-facing models, and ArtMentor studies how teachers work with a multimodal model in art assessment.

LLM safety

Models that take on sensitive roles such as teaching need to stay professionally sound and resist misuse. EduGuardBench and EMNLP measure the fidelity, ethics and adversarial safety of teacher-role models, and DoubtProbe defends black-box models against jailbreak attacks.

Currently exploring

How large language models relate to human language and to human cognition.

Other interests

LLM agents, recommender systems, and recursive self-improvement (RSI).

Conference Papers

  1. AAAI 2026 Oral CCF A

    EduGuardBench: A Holistic Benchmark for Evaluating the Pedagogical Fidelity and Adversarial Safety of LLMs as Simulated Teachers

    Yilin Jiang, Mingzi Zhang, Xuanyu Yin, Sheng Jin, Suyu Lu, Zuocan Ying, Zengyi Yu, Xiangjie Kong

    Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 40(37), 2026

    Evaluates teacher-role LLMs on professional fidelity, teaching harms and adversarial safety. Across 14 models, the safest ones turn harmful requests into teachable moments.

    Paper arXiv Code

  2. ACL 2026 Findings CCF A

    HACHIMI: Scalable and Controllable Student Persona Generation via Orchestrated Agents

    Yilin Jiang, Fei Tan, Xuanyu Yin, Leng Jing, Aimin Zhou

    Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2026

    A multi-agent framework that generates theory-aligned, distribution-controllable student personas, released as HACHIMI-1M with one million personas for Grades 1–12.

    Paper arXiv Code Dataset

  3. EMNLP 2026 Main CCF B

    ADE: Agentic Data Evolution Framework for Human-Centered Objectives

    Yang Yu*, Yilin Jiang*, Zexuan Fei, Yiming Luo, Xingkai Song, Kaiyi Huang, Aimin Zhou, Xin Lin, Fei Tan

    Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP), 2026

    Organizes synthetic supervision for weakly verifiable objectives as evolving data snapshots. On DEV300 it raises the extrinsic win rate after post-training from 55.2% to 68.9%.

    arXiv Code

  4. ICIC 2026 CCF C

    Do Large Language Models Suffer from Cognitive Overload? A Benchmark and Orchestration Framework

    Binghao Tu, Zengyi Yu, Sheng Jin, Yao Zhang, Mingwei Xu, Mingzi Zhang, Yilin Jiang, Xiaoqing Zhang, Jidong Weng, Xiaoyang Han

    International Conference on Intelligent Computing (ICIC), 2026

    Introduces CogniLoad, a benchmark of four types of cognitive load, and COF, a training-free multi-agent framework that improves LLM reasoning on high-load tasks.

    Paper Project

  5. EMNLP 2025 Oral CCF B

    EMNLP: Educator-role Moral and Normative Large Language Models Profiling

    Yilin Jiang, Mingzi Zhang, Sheng Jin, Zengyi Yu, Xiangjie Kong, Binghao Tu

    Proceedings of the 2025 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP)

    Compares 14 teacher-role LLMs with human teachers on personality, moral development and ethical risk. Models with stronger reasoning are more vulnerable to harmful prompt injection.

    Paper arXiv Code Dataset Project

  6. CHI 2025 CCF A

    ArtMentor: AI-Assisted Evaluation of Artworks to Explore Multimodal Large Language Models Capabilities

    Chanjin Zheng*, Zengyi Yu*, Yilin Jiang*, Mingzi Zhang, Xunuo Lu, Jing Jin, Liteng Gao

    Proceedings of the 2025 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems

    Pairs art teachers with a multimodal model to evaluate student artworks, recording 380 sessions across nine dimensions to assess how well MLLMs assist in art evaluation.

    Paper arXiv Project
