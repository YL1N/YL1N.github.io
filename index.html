About

I am an MPhil student in Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), where I am a member of the Di² Lab (Deep Interdisciplinary Intelligence Lab) led by Prof. Yutao Yue. I received my bachelor's degree in Educational Technology from Zhejiang University of Technology in 2025. Since October 2025 I have also been a research assistant at the Shanghai Innovation Institute and East China Normal University, where I work on InnoSpark, a Chinese educational large language model.

My research follows two main lines, AI for education and the safety of large language models. I am also exploring how language models relate to human language and cognition.

Research

AI for education

This line asks how large language models can serve teaching and learning. HACHIMI generates theory-aligned student personas with controllable ability and trait distributions, as a basis for simulating how a given learner answers. ELBench evaluates education-facing models on general capability, safety, basic education and high-level cultivation under one protocol. ADE organizes synthetic supervision for weakly verifiable educational objectives as evolving data snapshots. ArtMentor lets art teachers evaluate student artworks together with a multimodal model.

LLM safety

This line studies how language models behave under adversarial and ethically sensitive use, and how they can be defended. EduGuardBench measures the professional fidelity and adversarial safety of models that simulate teachers, and shows that the safest models turn harmful requests into teachable moments through educational refusals. EMNLP profiles the personality, moral development and ethical risk of teacher-role models against human teachers. DoubtProbe is a black-box jailbreak defense that combines structural verification with semantic auditing.

Currently exploring

How large language models relate to human language and to human cognition.
